# Finance milestone contract — 2026-11-04

Standalone implementation. Secretary/head manage term fees, invoices, payments and individual instalment agreements; directors/board see totals only; teachers have no finance access. Head alone approves financial assistance, opening balances and reversals. No live Paybill or bank connection.

Money is exact KES Decimal with two places, positive transaction amounts. Grade/term schedules have tuition and optional transport; invoice amounts are snapshots. One regular invoice per learner/term, requiring that year's enrolment and matching grade. No deletion or direct editing of issued invoices/payments. Head-approved invoice reversal cancels the balance and releases allocations back to their credit owners; the original remains and a corrected term invoice may be issued. Head-approved discounts/bursaries reduce an invoice's outstanding amount. Reversal of adjustments restores the original charge; reversal of payments invalidates all their allocations without deleting history.

Payments have unique receipt numbers, a named payer, a learner who owns any unallocated credit, optional initial invoice allocation, and cash/bank/M-Pesa method. Bank/M-Pesa require references; normalized references are unique per school/method even after reversal. Later allocations can split money between siblings' invoices within the same school, with a reason; amounts cannot exceed payment credit or invoice outstanding. Statements separate invoice debt and unallocated learner credit so money is counted once. Opening credits are head-approved carried-forward credit, excluded from new collection totals. No refunds or automatic reconciliation.

Numbering, allocations, approvals and monetary mutations serialize on the school row in one database transaction. Idempotency tokens prevent duplicate form replay. All writes recheck active membership and school ownership. Every mutation writes an event; approval requests show requester, reason, reviewer and outcome. Invalid input has no partial financial effects.

Instalment plans are append-only revisions of an invoice agreement: ordered due dates/amounts total the current net invoice charge, with an agreed-with name and notes. They do not create charges. Current net charge changes mark an existing agreement as needing revision; don't falsely report it as current. FIFO allocation of invoice payments to due instalments gives remaining/overdue amounts. Manual payment entry only, no statement import in this milestone.

Acceptance: arithmetic/reversal/credit reconciliation, duplicate submissions/references, invoice price snapshots, approval boundaries, tenant isolation across forms/services/detail views, sibling allocation, instalment overdue/revision behavior, printable receipt/statement and mobile flows. SQLite local tests; PostgreSQL concurrency requires separate environment verification before pilot.
